The Idea

You already told me what this is for.

On the phone you said the website and social stuff needs some love, so you could be busier. That is the whole job, and it is the only thing I am measuring this against.

"We are busy, which is good, but we also know that a lot of our social media and website stuff needs a lot of love. It needs some love so we could be busier."

You are not starting from a weak spot. Both farms are booked, Dulany's and Walker's are running neck and neck this year, and couples are finding you through Instagram and through Google. What they are not doing is finding you through the website. By your own count, about seven out of every ten inquiries come from somewhere else. That gap is the opportunity, and it is the number I want to move.

Right now Walker's and Dulany's share one address and one story, and they are not one story. Walker's is a tent for 275 on a hardwood floor, a walnut tree to stand under, a stone colonial house, and the mancave. Dulany's is a barn for 200, a rose garden or the woodland, a barn bar, and 170 acres around it. Those are two completely different weddings. A couple choosing between them should feel that difference in about four seconds, and right now they cannot.

So here is the plan. Two sites, on the two addresses you already own, each telling its own farm's story with its own photographs and its own inquiry form landing in your inbox. Eight coordinators who handle everything, said out loud instead of buried. And when the Hagerstown partnership is ready, it gets its own site, standing completely on its own, exactly the way you described it.

The Fine Print

To book: a third to start, a third at the first look in December, a third at launch. On Both Overlooks that is $4,167 three times.

This quote holds for: 30 days from the date on the cover.

Photos: I will need a short written OK from each photographer who contributes, even just an email reply saying the venues can use their images on the websites. Photographers own their wedding photos even when it is your property in the frame, and venues get caught out on this more often than you would think. Every contributing photographer gets credit on the site, which is usually why they say yes.

If photos run late: the schedule moves, the payment dates do not. Projects like this stall waiting on images more than anything else, which is why November 21 is in here on purpose.

Revisions: two full rounds on each site are included, which is more than almost anyone uses. Past that it is $95 an hour.
