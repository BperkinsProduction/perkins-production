01 Where things stand today

The work is great. The website hides it.

I went through twelvewestdetail.com on a computer and on my phone. Your photos are some of the best I have seen from a detailer around here, and you were voted the best in Frederick County. The site makes people work to find any of that.

✦GoDaddy runs an ad for its own website builder across the top of your site. It is the first thing every customer reads.
✦On a phone, the first screen is your logo and empty dark space. No photo, no prices, no button to book.
✦The page opens with a form that only asks for a name, email and phone. Every request turns into a call or a text to find out what car, what package and what day.
✦Every price is Small, Mid, Large or XL, but nothing says which size a car is. That is one more question you answer by text.
✦Your 2020 Best of the Best win is nowhere on the site, and Google sees a page titled "twelve west detail" with no description, so searches like "detailing Walkersville" have little to go on.
02 What you get

Everything in the demo, built for real

A website built for phones

Your logo, your bay, your photos from the site and Facebook. Packages, ceramic coating, recent work, interiors, specialty vehicles, hours and directions, with a call button that stays at the bottom of the screen.

Prices that answer the question

Your full price list, all 5 packages from the $85 Ceramic Wash to the $645 Platinum Plus. Tap a vehicle size and every price on the page changes, with example cars so people know which size they are.

A quote that builds itself

Customers pick a size, a package and any of your add ons, like pet hair from $99 or headlights at $40 each, and see the total. Add ons a package already covers show as included, so nobody pays twice.

Booking requests that make sense

The request comes to your phone and email as one message: name, number, year make and model, the package, the price and the day they want. You text back a time. Sundays are blocked.

Ceramic deposits online

Coating jobs hold the day with your 20% non refundable deposit, paid by card when they book. On a $775 coating that is $155 in hand before the car shows up.

Found on Google

A real title and description, your award and your town on the page, and your Google listing pointed at the new site. Your domain stays twelvewestdetail.com.
