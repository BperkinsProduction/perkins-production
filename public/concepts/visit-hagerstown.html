If it is your first visit

Start with these

Eight places locals send their out-of-town family. Hours and details come from each listing, so they stay current.

Browse all attractions
Sharpsburg · History

Antietam National Battlefield

The bloodiest single day in American history, kept as open farmland. Drive the loop, then walk Burnside Bridge.

Open daily, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Grounds open daylight hours.
Williamsport · Outdoors

C&O Canal National Historical Park

184.5 miles of towpath along the Potomac. Cushwa Basin in Williamsport is the easy way in, with a visitor center in the old warehouse.

Towpath open daily. Visitor center hours vary by season.
Hagerstown · Arts

Washington County Museum of Fine Arts

A real art museum on the lake in City Park, with a permanent collection and rotating shows. Admission is free.

Tuesday to Sunday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Closed Mondays.
Boonsboro · Outdoors

Greenbrier State Park

A 42-acre lake with a swimming beach, and the Appalachian Trail passing right through the park.

Open daily. Day-use fee in season.
Wine
Rohrersville · Wine

Big Cork Vineyards

One hundred rolling acres in Pleasant Valley, with a tasting room that fills up on fall weekends.

Friday to Monday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Downtown · Arts

The Maryland Theatre

The 1915 theater that anchors the Arts and Entertainment District. Home of the Maryland Symphony Orchestra.

Box office Monday to Friday, 11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
Caverns
Boonsboro · Family

Crystal Grottoes Caverns

Maryland's only show cave, a few minutes from the battlefield. Cool underground on a hot day, and the tour is short enough for kids.

Daily April to November, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Downtown · Eat

Schmankerl Stube

A Bavarian restaurant on South Potomac Street for more than 36 years. Sit on the patio and order the schnitzel.

Tuesday to Saturday, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday to 9 p.m.

Follow a trail

The county has already done the route planning. Each trail is a printable map, a drive, and a list of stops that are open.

  • Pottery Trail

    Ten working potters along the South Mountain corridor, most with studios you can walk into.

    →

  • Stone Arch Bridges

    More than 21 historic stone bridges, with a brochure that strings them into one drive.

    →

  • Grapes & Grains

    Wineries, breweries and a distillery across the county, with a designated driver in the plan.

    →

  • Antietam Creek Water Trail

    Forty-one miles of Class I and II paddling through farmland and the battlefield park.

    →

  • Three Scenic Byways

    The Antietam Campaign, the C&O Canal, and the Historic National Road.

    →
