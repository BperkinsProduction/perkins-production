Your first visit

First time? Here's the whole thing.

  1. Walk in.

    Stop by either salon. Bring a photo ID.

  2. Tell us how you burn.

    We'll match you to a level, or to a spray tan if you'd rather skip UV.

  3. Short and private.

    Your own room, goggles on, and a short first session. Wear whatever you're comfortable in.

  4. Build your color.

    Color builds over a few visits. Give your skin a day between sessions.

Age rules. UV tanning is 17 and up in Pennsylvania, and 17-year-olds need a parent's written OK. For prom, ask us about spray tans.

I burn easy. Can I still tan?

Tell us at the desk. We'll start you low and short, or put you in the Mystic booth, which uses no UV at all.

Which bed should I pick?

Higher levels are stronger, so they need fewer minutes. Most people start at Level 1 or 2. Try the level finder above, or just ask us.

Will a spray tan make me look orange?

Not if we match your shade. Exfoliate and shave the day before, come in without lotion, deodorant, or makeup, and follow the poses in the booth. Color shows in 4 to 6 hours and lasts up to 7 days.

Am I locked into a contract?

You don't have to be. Every plan gives you the choice: $0 down, or no commitment at all.

Do I have to buy lotion?

No. Lotion helps your color and keeps skin from drying out, and we're glad to point you to a good one. It's your call.

How often should I tan?

Give your skin at least a day between UV sessions, and never tan to the point of burning. Unlimited means you set the schedule, not that more is better.

What do I wear?

The rooms are private, so wear whatever you're comfortable in. Goggles go on every time.

Does red light tan me?

No. Red light uses no UV and won't change your color.

Is the gym really included?

Yes. Every Sunbodies plan comes with a Sports Inn Fitness membership.
